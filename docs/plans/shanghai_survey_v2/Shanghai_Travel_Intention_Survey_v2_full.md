## 调查说明 / About this survey

您好！我们正在研究在上海出行的人如何根据时间、费用、天气和公共交通条件作出选择。问卷包括个人出行情况、一段可跳过的实际出行回忆，以及10个假设情景。

Hello! We are studying how people who travel in Shanghai make choices based on time, cost, weather and public transport conditions. The survey covers your travel circumstances, an optional account of an actual trip, and 10 hypothetical situations.

本调查预计需要约15–20分钟，这是试填前的估计。参与完全自愿，您可以跳过不愿回答的问题，也可以随时退出。题目没有标准答案，请按自己的情况和偏好回答。

The survey is provisionally estimated to take 15–20 minutes. Participation is voluntary. You may skip questions you do not wish to answer or stop at any time. There are no right or wrong preference answers.

我们不要求姓名、身份证号、电话号码或门牌地址。答案会使用研究编号关联，用于去标识后的个体选择分析和模型预测比较；公开报告以汇总结果为主。位置问题可选择不提供，请不要填写住址、单位名称或其他可识别个人的信息。有关数据处理的具体说明和研究联系方式，见您收到的招募邀请或调查平台的信息页。

We do not ask for your name, identity number, telephone number or street address. Responses are linked by a study ID and used for de-identified individual choice analysis and comparisons with model predictions. Public reports mainly present aggregate results. Location questions are optional: do not provide a home address, employer name or other identifying details. Data-handling information and research contact details are available in the recruitment invitation or the survey information page.

## 四、10个假设情景 / 4. Ten hypothetical situations

### 所有情景共同的出行任务 / The same trip task applies throughout

请设想在上海的一个普通工作日，您需要**本人前往一家商店领取已预约的物品，最晚09:00到店，过时当天无法领取；此次不使用配送或代领**。您原计划08:00出发，起点到商店约6公里。

Imagine an ordinary weekday in Shanghai. You need to **collect a reserved item from a shop in person by 09:00; collection later that day is unavailable. Delivery and collection by someone else are not options for this trip.** You originally planned to leave at 08:00. The shop is about 6 km from your starting point.

您在前一晚20:00已经获知各情景所述信息，可以据此调整计划。在06:00–10:00之间，假定该情景中的天气、费用和表内预计总时间保持不变。因此，提早或推迟出发不会自动使道路更通畅、票价更低或雨停。提前到达可以在店外有遮蔽处等候。

You learned the stated conditions at 20:00 the previous evening and can adjust your plan. From 06:00 to 10:00, assume that the weather, costs and predicted total times in each situation remain unchanged. Leaving earlier or later does not automatically reduce congestion or fares or make the rain stop. If you arrive early, sheltered waiting is available outside the shop.

### 方式与费用说明 / Modes and costs

- 仅比较**自驾小汽车、公共交通、普通人力自行车、步行**。这组假设任务不提供出租车、网约车、电动车或共享单车。请按题目条件作答；前面的实际出行答案无需与此相同。
  Compare only **driving a private car, public transport, a conventional pedal bicycle and walking**. Taxi, ride-hailing, electric bicycles and shared bicycles are not offered for these hypothetical trips. Your earlier actual-travel answers need not match these choices.
- 自驾只有在您有家庭车辆、有驾照且能安排本人使用时可选；普通自行车只有在您有家庭普通自行车且能亲自使用时可选。不可用的方式显示为灰色，不能选。给定路线允许题中可用车辆通行。
  Driving is selectable only if you have a personal/household car, a licence and access to drive it. Cycling is selectable only if you have a personal/household conventional bicycle that you can use yourself. Unavailable modes are greyed out and cannot be selected. The route permits the available vehicles.
- 未确认可用的自驾或自行车，本组题目也不提供；页面会先显示您的可用方式，请确认后继续。
  Driving or cycling whose availability has not been confirmed is also not offered. The survey first shows your offered modes for you to confirm.
