## Ethics and consent statement

The surveys were administered anonymously, and participants received study information and provided electronic consent before taking part. Participation was voluntary, and no direct personal identifiers were collected. Only de-identified attributes required for the model analyses were transmitted to the external model service. The study was conducted without a formal institutional ethics review or exemption determination.
